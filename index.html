Personal Finance Hub

A small set of calculators to help you understand the mechanics of your own money — compounding, debt, and the true cost of a mortgage. Nothing here tells you what to do with your money. It just shows you what your own numbers are actually doing.
No data is ever collected. Every calculator here runs entirely in your browser — nothing you type is sent, saved, or shared anywhere. If you want to keep your numbers, use the Export button inside each tool to save a file to your own device.
