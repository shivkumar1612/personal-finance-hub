Mortgage Calculator

Personal Finance Hub — see what your own numbers do, not what you should do with them.

Mortgage providers and financial news almost always focus on one number: the interest rate. Two other factors get far less attention — the term (how long you take to repay) and whether you make any prepayments — even though changing either can have a bigger effect on the total interest you actually pay a bank than a modest rate difference does.

This calculator shows you that effect using your own numbers. It doesn't tell you what to do with them.

Important: the prepayment scenarios below assume any extra payment reduces your term, not your monthly payment. Most UK lenders do not do this automatically — you usually need to explicitly instruct your bank (a form or a phone call) to apply overpayments to reduce the term rather than just sitting as a credit.
Not financial advice. This tool is for illustration only, built to help you understand the mechanics of your own numbers.
