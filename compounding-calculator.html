Personal Finance Hub — see what your own numbers do, not what you should do with them.

Markets crash. Markets recover. In between, most people panic — selling during COVID, wars, downturns — trying to time moves that even professionals get wrong more often than they get right.

The single biggest driver of long-term wealth usually isn't picking the right moment to buy or sell. It's patience — staying invested, and starting as early as possible. This calculator shows you exactly what patience is worth, in your own numbers.

Not financial advice. This assumes a constant annual return, which real markets never actually deliver smoothly — real returns vary year to year. It's a simplification to illustrate the mechanics of compounding, not a prediction or recommendation. The creator is not a qualified financial advisor. Please speak to a regulated financial professional before making any real decisions.
