Cost of a Habit Calculator

Personal Finance Hub — see what your own numbers do, not what you should do with them.

Small recurring costs rarely feel like they matter — a coffee here, a takeaway there. Individually, none of them seem worth thinking about.

But money spent today is money that can no longer compound. This calculator shows any regular habit two ways: what it actually costs you in cash, and what that same money could have been worth if it had been invested instead. Both numbers are real — it's your call which one matters more to you.

Not financial advice. The "invested value" assumes a constant annual return, which real markets never actually deliver smoothly — real returns vary year to year, and this is not a prediction. This tool doesn't tell you whether to keep or cut a habit — it just shows you the numbers behind it. The creator is not a qualified financial advisor. Please speak to a regulated financial professional before making any real decisions.
